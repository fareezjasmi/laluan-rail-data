Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026

The short version

FocusTrack does not collect your personal data, does not track you, and has no ads or analytics. Your rides, focus history, profile and settings stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your journey log, focus times, streaks, the name and avatar you choose, your settings and the timetables you download are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase your journey log in Settings at any time.

What the app connects to, and why

  • Map tiles from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org), to draw the map. Like any web request, the tile server sees your device's IP address. No account or identifier is sent.
  • Timetable packs from GitHub (github.com), where we publish open rail timetables. GitHub sees your IP address when a pack downloads.
  • Purchases are made through Apple. If you subscribe to Premium, Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to confirm your subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase history, solely to unlock Premium on your devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not sell, share or rent data, and nothing is used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, FocusTrack schedules them on your device: one when your train arrives, and an optional daily reminder you can turn off in Settings. They are not sent from a server.

Live Activities and sounds

The Lock Screen Live Activity and the train and weather sounds run entirely on your device.

Children

FocusTrack is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
