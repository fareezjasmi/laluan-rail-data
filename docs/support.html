Common questions

How does FocusTrack pick a train?

It looks at the real timetable for the station you're at and finds trains whose running time is close to the focus length you chose. When you arrive, your next ride starts from where you got off.

Are the trains live?

No. Times come from the published timetable (one real, typical weekday), not live running information, so your ride is never delayed.

Which countries are available?

Malaysia, Germany, France, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Belgium and Spain. Tap the country button on the home screen to open the globe and download one. Timetables refresh automatically every week.

Why are there no trains from my station?

Some stations only have trains at certain times or are served by lines missing from the open data. Tap Change under "You're at" to pick another station.

What does Premium include?

City, suburban and regional trains of any length are free. Premium adds long-distance trains: high-speed (ICE, TGV, AVE and more), ETS, Intercity and night trains. It's a yearly subscription with a free trial for new subscribers.

How do I cancel or restore Premium?

Cancel any time in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. On a new phone, open FocusTrack › Profile › Settings › Restore purchases.

What is Deep Focus?

An optional mode: if you leave the app for more than 15 seconds during a ride, you miss your train. Locking your phone doesn't count. Turn it on in Settings.

The Live Activity isn't showing

Check Settings › FocusTrack › Live Activities is on, and that "Live Activity on the Lock Screen" is on in FocusTrack's own Settings.

Can I use my own music?

Yes. FocusTrack's sounds mix with other apps, and you can turn the train and weather sounds off from the speaker button on the ride screen.
